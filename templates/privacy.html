Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password. When you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, to save your expenses, and to show you summaries and spending patterns. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on the servers that run Spendly. Passwords are stored in protected form, not as plain text. We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, but no system is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address. We do not share your expense data with third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact us at the support address listed on the Spendly website.
